# [EXEMPLO] Andrej Karpathy

## Who/What

Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher and educator — former Director of AI at Tesla, founding team at OpenAI, and one of the clearest technical communicators in the field. Known for his zero-to-hero neural-network courses and for making deep learning legible to a wide audience.

In early 2026 he publicly described using an LLM to maintain a personal wiki: feeding the model his accumulated notes and asking it to write, link, and update knowledge pages on his behalf. He called this a "wiki-llm" or "LLM wiki." AXI25 is built on this exact pattern — the agent as wiki curator, not just assistant.

## Relevant To

- [[map-knowledge-management]] — his "wiki-llm" idea is the conceptual ancestor of AXI25 agent model
- [[atomic-notes]] — the wiki-llm pattern depends on atomic, linkable notes; a blob of text cannot be maintained by an agent

## Key Facts

- Coined (or popularised) the framing "LLM as wiki curator" in the 2026 discourse around personal knowledge management
- His pedagogical style — teach by building, minimise abstraction, show the full stack — is a useful model for knowledge note design
- Published a viral thread on the limits of passive reading and the superiority of active retrieval (aligns with spaced-repetition evidence)
